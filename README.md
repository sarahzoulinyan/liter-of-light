# Liter of Light · While Chasing Light, Become a Light

> While chasing light, I also want to become a light.

Growing up in rural Philippines, I knew how much a single lamp could mean to a family. In many areas, limited access to electricity means families cannot simply turn on a light at night. That is why we founded **Liter of Light**.

We collect old electronics and reuse solar panels, batteries and LEDs. Using our engineering skills, we turn them into portable lights for families without reliable electricity. So far, we have built over 500 lights. We work with local schools and community centers to bring them to families in need, and we hold workshops so people can learn to make and repair their own lights.

In one village, a child told me that our lamp helped him read at night for the first time. In another, a grandmother said she could finally sew clothes after dark for her family. We realized that we were bringing not just a liter of light, but hope to an entire community.

## 项目与设计原则

- **先解决真实需求**：光线要稳定，让孩子阅读、家人缝纫或完成夜间家务。
- **安全地再利用**：旧电芯和电子元件必须检测；腐蚀、破损、鼓包或容量异常的电芯不能继续使用。
- **让社区能够维修**：使用常见工具、标准元件和可拆结构，并在学校及社区中心传授制作方法。
- **适应菲律宾环境**：考虑高温、潮湿、雨季、运输距离和日照变化，保护电路与接头。
- **用更少能源获得更长照明**：高效 LED、光学扩散和电池管理共同延长续航。
